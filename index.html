About

I am a graduate student in the School of Computer Science at Nanjing University and the State Key Laboratory for Novel Software Technology. My research combines large language models with formal reasoning to make verification more automated, with recent work on inductive invariant inference, counterexample-guided candidate synthesis, and machine-assisted proof development for distributed systems.
